# 90-Second Demo Script — Ikigai Royal Map

*Spoken pace: calm, unhurried. Timings are guides. All names below are demo sample data.*

**0–10s — The problem.**
“Most advice about finding your purpose is just… more reading. A student, a caregiver, a retiree — they don't need another article. They need something they can *do* in five minutes, in their own language, without signing up for anything.”

**10–20s — The inspiration.**
“I was inspired by the ideas in García and Miralles' book *Ikigai* — purpose, flow, community, mindfulness, resilience. But I didn't copy the book. I asked: what if each idea became something you can touch? That's what I built — an original, independent interpretation, in English and Tamil.”

**20–35s — What I built.**
“It's called Ikigai Royal Map. Forty gentle questions produce your personal pattern map. A flow lab measures what absorbs you. A journal, a 30-day plan, and a support circle turn insight into action. No accounts, no tracking — everything lives privately in your browser.”

**35–60s — The journey.** *(Click through live.)*
“Watch: I hit ‘Try with sample data’ — real data is snapshotted first, nothing is lost. Here's a completed assessment… the radar map, strongest and growth areas… three tiny experiments, not life commands. The journal holds private reflections. The plan breaks purpose into one small step a day — and skipping a day is fine here; it's data, not failure.”

**60–75s — The result.** *(Open results + dashboard.)*
“The payoff is this: a printable, exportable snapshot — your pattern, your experiments, your next step. Switch to Tamil — the *entire* app follows, essays included. Export everything as one versioned file you own.”

**75–90s — What makes it different.**
“Three things: fully bilingual down to the essays; privacy as architecture, not a setting; and honest scoring — ‘not sure’ stays unscored, incomplete results say ‘provisional’. Fifty-four browser tests and twenty-two unit tests keep it that way. Thank you — try the demo yourself, it takes sixty seconds.”
